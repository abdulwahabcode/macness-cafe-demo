Our story

Unpretentious, warm
& proudly local.

Macness is a classic family-run Scottish cafe — the kind of place where the regulars have their own table and newcomers are regulars by the second visit. No fuss, no frills: just generous portions, fair prices and food made with care.

From a proper full Scottish breakfast to a steaming bowl of homemade soup at lunchtime, everything here is about value and comfort. For many in the neighbourhood, Macness isn't just a cafe — it's the hub of the community.
